# Third-party pack notices

**Perry the Pug** is the exception to everything below: an original
character, depicting no existing intellectual property, contributed to this
repository rather than converted from someone else's art. Nothing in this
file's warnings applies to it.

His sprites were produced with ChatGPT's image model from original prompts
describing pug anatomy — no existing character, franchise, or artist was named
in them, and no third-party art was used as a source or reference. Stated
plainly because it changes the licensing basis rather than because it is a
caveat: generated images are widely held not to attract copyright for want of
human authorship, so rather than being licensed to this project, Perry's art is
most likely subject to no copyright anyone can assert. That makes him freer to
redistribute than every pack listed below, not less free — but it is a
different footing from art an author owns and licenses, and worth knowing
before the pack is shipped.

The other eighteen packs: the default cat is self-generated, and the
seventeen remaining are third-party fan art converted with this plugin's
`tools/import-*.py` converters, with author credit kept in each pack's
`pack.json`. All character names belong to their respective owners; the
packs are included as non-commercial fan art for personal use, and a
rights holder may request removal at any time.
